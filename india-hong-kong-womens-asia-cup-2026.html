India Storm Into Asia Cup Semifinals After Hong Kong Win

4 September 2026

India secured their place in the semi-finals of the Women’s Asia Cup with a commanding 137-run victory over Hong Kong in Dubai.

India made 193 for 5, led by Smriti Mandhana’s extraordinary 124 from 64 balls. She struck 14 fours and seven sixes in an innings that combined control with aggressive strokeplay.

The bowlers then completed the job. Hong Kong were dismissed for 56 in 16.2 overs, with Deepti Sharma taking three wickets. India’s all-round performance made the result one of the clearest wins of the tournament so far.

The victory also means India can approach their final group match against Pakistan with qualification already secured, while Pakistan face greater pressure in the race for the remaining semi-final places.

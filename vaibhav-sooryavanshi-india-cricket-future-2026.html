Vaibhav Sooryavanshi and India’s Next Generation of Batting Talent

5 September 2026

Indian cricket continues to invest attention in its next generation, and teenager Vaibhav Sooryavanshi has become one of the most discussed young batters in the current setup.

After a difficult England tour, Sooryavanshi responded with two half-centuries in India’s 3-0 T20I series win over Zimbabwe. His performances have strengthened the case for giving young players carefully managed opportunities at international level.

The challenge now is development rather than rushing a teenager into every assignment. Technical growth, workload management and exposure to different conditions will matter as much as headline-making innings.

India’s depth in batting is one of its biggest strengths. The emergence of young players such as Sooryavanshi gives the national setup another option while the selectors plan for the next cycle.

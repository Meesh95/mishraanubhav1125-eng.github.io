Why India-Pakistan Remains a Major Women’s Cricket Rivalry

5 September 2026

The India-Pakistan women’s cricket fixture carries significance beyond the group table. Every meeting attracts attention because the rivalry combines sporting history, regional interest and high-pressure tournament cricket.

India enter the 5 September Women’s Asia Cup meeting in Dubai having already qualified for the semi-finals after wins over Thailand and Hong Kong. Pakistan, meanwhile, need a strong result as they fight to keep their qualification hopes alive.

India’s historical record is heavily in their favour. Across 29 women’s international meetings, India have won 26 and Pakistan three. In T20Is, India lead 14-3, while India have won all 12 ODI meetings.

Those numbers provide context, but they do not remove the pressure of a rivalry match. Pakistan’s incentive is clear: challenge India, collect points and keep the tournament campaign alive.

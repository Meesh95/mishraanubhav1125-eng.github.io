Manu Bhaker Leads India’s Shooting Challenge at Asian Games

5 September 2026

Manu Bhaker is among the leading names in India’s shooting squad for the 2026 Asian Games in Aichi-Nagoya.

The Olympic medallist has been selected for both the women’s 10m air pistol and 25m pistol events. The selection reflects India’s strategy of using athletes capable of competing across more than one event under the Games’ participation limits.

Rudrankksh Patil is another Indian shooter selected for two events, while Anish Bhanwala is set to be India’s representative in the men’s 25m rapid-fire pistol.

Shooting has historically been an important medal source for India at major multi-sport events. The Asian Games will test the country’s depth against the continent’s strongest shooting programmes.

India’s Asian Games Contingent Expands With Surfing Debut

5 September 2026

India’s participation at the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games has expanded with surfing added to the country’s programme, taking India’s sanctioned contingent to 768 members.

The revised athlete strength stands at 503 sportspersons, including 269 men and 234 women. Surfing brings four Indian competitors: Kishore Kumar Anbarashu and Sivaraj Babu in the men’s shortboard, and Kamali Prakash and Sugar Shanti Gayen in the women’s shortboard.

The Games run from 19 September to 4 October. The broader programme gives India opportunities to compete for medals across both established disciplines and newer sports.

For Indian sport, the surfing entry is also significant as it widens the national footprint beyond traditional medal events and gives emerging athletes a continental platform.

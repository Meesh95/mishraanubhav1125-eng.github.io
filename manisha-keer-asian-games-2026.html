Manisha Keer’s Road From Borrowed Equipment to Asian Games Dream

5 September 2026

For Indian trap shooter Manisha Keer, the journey to the Asian Games began without the advantages normally associated with elite shooting.

Growing up in Bhopal, Keer trained using borrowed rifles before progressing through the competitive system. Her persistence has since taken her to World Cup medals and an Asian Games silver medal in 2023.

At 27, she now returns to the Asian Games with greater experience and a clear target: convert international consistency into a gold medal in Aichi-Nagoya.

Her story highlights a less visible side of Indian sport—the athletes who build elite careers through gradual progress, family sacrifice and years of training before the biggest stages arrive.

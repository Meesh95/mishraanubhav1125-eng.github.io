Why VVS Laxman Will Coach India at the Asian Games

5 September 2026

VVS Laxman will lead India’s coaching staff for the men’s cricket campaign at the 2026 Asian Games in Aichi-Nagoya, Japan.

The appointment reflects the unusual scheduling demands around the tournament. India’s Asian Games campaign overlaps with other international commitments, making it difficult for Gautam Gambhir and his regular support staff to take charge.

Laxman brings extensive experience from the National Cricket Academy and has previously worked with Indian squads on assignments involving younger and fringe players.

His role will be particularly important because the Asian Games provide an opportunity to test India’s depth in a multi-sport environment. Managing a squad that includes established names and emerging players will require a different approach from a normal bilateral series.

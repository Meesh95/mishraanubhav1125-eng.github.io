Global Chess League Returns to Bengaluru With World-Class Field

5 September 2026

The fourth season of the Tech Mahindra Global Chess League begins in Bengaluru on 5 September, bringing six franchise teams together for a rapid-format competition.

The tournament features a double round-robin league stage followed by a best-of-two final, with a total prize pool of $1 million. The field includes major names such as Viswanathan Anand, Arjun Erigaisi, Koneru Humpy, Harika Dronavalli and Divya Deshmukh, alongside international stars.

Magnus Carlsen returns as part of the Alpine APL Pipers, while India’s established players give the Bengaluru event a strong home connection.

With the tournament combining team strategy and rapid chess, the league offers Indian fans a different way to follow elite players compared with traditional individual events.

Yashvir Singh Steps In as Neeraj Chopra Misses Asian Games

5 September 2026

India’s athletics squad for the 2026 Asian Games has changed after javelin star Neeraj Chopra was ruled unavailable because of injury.

Commonwealth Games bronze medallist Yashvir Singh has been brought into the 77-member athletics team. The Athletics Federation of India also announced changes elsewhere in the squad, including the withdrawal of long-distance runner Abhishek Pal following a notice from the National Anti-Doping Agency over an alleged doping offence.

Chopra’s absence removes one of India’s biggest medal prospects, but the athletics team still has considerable depth and several athletes looking to make a breakthrough.

The Asian Games begin on 19 September in Aichi-Nagoya, making the final weeks of preparation crucial for athletes trying to peak at the right time.

India Women vs Pakistan Women: Another Chapter in the Asia Cup Rivalry

5 September 2026 · Dubai, UAE

India Women face Pakistan Women in the ACC Women’s Asia Cup 2026 on Saturday, with another high-profile chapter of one of women’s cricket’s most watched rivalries set to unfold in Dubai.

The two teams have met 29 times in women’s international cricket. India have won 26 of those encounters, while Pakistan have won 3. The rivalry has been particularly one-sided in 50-over cricket, where India have won all 12 of their ODI meetings.

T20I record

In 17 women's T20I meetings, India lead Pakistan 14–3. That record gives India the historical edge, although a single T20 match can turn quickly through a strong powerplay, a match-winning spell or a late hitting surge.

ODI record

India's ODI record against Pakistan is even stronger: 12 wins from 12 meetings. The record reflects India's long-term dominance in the format.

Why this match matters

India entered the match having qualified for the semifinals after wins over Thailand and Hong Kong. Pakistan, meanwhile, needed a positive result to keep their qualification hopes alive, adding importance to the contest beyond the rivalry itself.

With the match being played in Dubai, conditions, spin through the middle overs and the ability to handle pressure in a high-profile fixture could all prove decisive.

Head-to-head at a glance
Overall: India 26–3 Pakistan
T20Is: India 14–3 Pakistan
ODIs: India 12–0 Pakistan

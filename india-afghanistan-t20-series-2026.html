India Name Full-Strength Squad for Afghanistan T20I Series

5 September 2026

India are set to host Afghanistan in a three-match T20I series in Delhi beginning 13 September, giving the side an important competitive window before the Asian Games.

The selection has attracted attention because the squad is close to full strength. Jasprit Bumrah, Harshit Rana, Nitish Kumar Reddy and Washington Sundar are among the players selected, while Shreyas Iyer leads the team.

The decision has also prompted debate over workload and fitness, particularly with several selected players expected to feature in India’s Asian Games campaign later in September.

For the selectors, the series offers a chance to build momentum, assess combinations and give players meaningful T20 cricket before the continental tournament.

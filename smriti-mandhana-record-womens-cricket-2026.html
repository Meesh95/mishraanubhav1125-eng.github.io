Smriti Mandhana Breaks Women’s International Runs Record

4 September 2026

Smriti Mandhana has moved to the top of the women’s international run-scoring charts after a spectacular innings against Hong Kong in the Women’s Asia Cup.

Mandhana made 124 from 64 balls, striking 14 fours and seven sixes, as India posted 193 for 5 before bowling Hong Kong out for 56. The 137-run victory also sent India into the tournament semi-finals.

The milestone is especially significant because it came in an attacking T20I innings rather than a long accumulation of runs. Mandhana has now built a career record that combines consistency with an increasingly aggressive scoring style.

Her latest achievement takes her past Mithali Raj’s previous women’s international aggregate of 10,868 runs. It also marked Mandhana’s 18th international century, moving her ahead of previous leaders in that category.

For Indian cricket, the record is another marker of how far the women’s game has grown. Mandhana’s longevity, three-format success and ability to produce high-impact innings have made her one of India’s central sporting figures.

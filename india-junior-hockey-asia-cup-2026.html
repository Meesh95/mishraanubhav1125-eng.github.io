India’s Junior Hockey Programme Looks to Build Senior-Level Habits

5 September 2026

India’s junior hockey setup is placing increasing emphasis on preparing young players for the demands of senior international hockey.

Coach Tim White has stressed that the next generation needs more than technical ability. Professional habits, discipline, work ethic and mental preparation are being treated as essential parts of development.

The approach is designed to make the transition from age-group hockey to the senior national team smoother. Young players who learn professional standards early can arrive at the next level better prepared for pressure and tactical demands.

For a country with a deep hockey tradition, developing a reliable pathway from juniors to seniors remains one of the most important long-term priorities.
